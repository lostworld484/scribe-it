Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Scribe-It is a free drawing and guessing game you play with friends in your browser. This page explains what information the game uses, why, and how long it is kept. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no selling of data, and game data is deleted soon after you stop playing.

What we collect

  • What you choose to share: the nickname and avatar you pick, the room code you join, and what you do in a game: drawings, chat messages, guesses, reactions, drawing ratings and scores.
  • A guest ID: when you open the game you are given a random, anonymous guest ID so the game can recognise you if you reconnect. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Connection status: whether you are online in a room and when you were last seen, so your spot can be held for 60 seconds if you disconnect.

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

What is visible to other players

Everyone in the same room can see your nickname, avatar, drawings, chat messages, reactions and score. Anyone with the room link or code can join a room, so please don't share personal details in chat or drawings.

Where it is stored and who handles it

  • Google Firebase runs the live game (database and guest sign-in). Game data is stored on Firebase's servers in Singapore. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Vercel hosts the website and, like any web host, may keep short-lived technical logs such as IP addresses to keep the site running and secure.
  • Google Fonts provides the lettering, so your browser contacts Google's font servers when the page loads.

We don't use advertising, tracking cookies or analytics, and we don't sell or rent any information.

How long it is kept

  • Drawings, guesses and ratings for a turn are deleted when the next turn starts.
  • A whole room, including its chat and scores, is deleted after about 24 hours without activity.
  • Guest IDs that are more than 30 days old may be deleted automatically.
  • Results cards are made on your own device and only leave it if you choose to share them.

On your device

The game saves a few settings in your browser's local storage so you don't have to re-enter them: your nickname, avatar, sound setting and the last room you were in. You can clear these at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Children

Scribe-It is meant for people aged 13 and over. Players under 18 should only play with a parent's or guardian's permission, and should play in rooms with people they know.

Your choices

  • You can leave a room at any time with the Leave button.
  • You can clear the data saved on your device through your browser settings.
  • You can ask for data linked to your guest ID to be deleted by using the contact details below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have a question about this page, you can reach the operator of Scribe-It through the person who invited you to play.
